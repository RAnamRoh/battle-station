# Marketing and art rules
- Original IP only: never name or imitate a franchise, studio, game, artist or celebrity in prompts, scripts or visuals.
- Real coworkers appear only with recorded consent. Mark AI-generated likenesses ("AI-generated art") in public material.
- Renders and raw captures are not committed (they are ignored). Record uploads in a manifest.
- Video compositions: use the hyperframes skills. One folder per video in `marketing/compositions/`.
- Art follows the current style bible in `art/style/`. Every generated image gets a `*.gen.json` provenance sidecar.
